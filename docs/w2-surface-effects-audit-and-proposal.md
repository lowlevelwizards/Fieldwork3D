## Appearance vocabulary: intrinsic material structure (always independent of age)

- Painted plaster/drywall: nearly flat fields, optional intentional two-tone lower paint band, small palette variation by room; no ubiquitous speckle. 
- Masonry: brick/cinderblock staggered physically sized courses, one restrained mortar line, occasional block value variation. Concrete has larger construction joints only where appropriate.
- Metal panels: broad vertical or horizontal seams, optional painted industrial safety/wayfinding band; rare restrained rib highlight. Siding uses overlapping horizontal courses; wood grain restrained and oriented. Tiles (wet room face) use clear grout; no tiled-dungeon defaults.
- Baseboard/trim, sill/head/corner and crown: color accents separate planes graphically; genuine projecting edges remain geometry until W4. Painted stripe, kick band and fine seams never require extruded geometry. Real openings, frames and damageable geometry unchanged.
- Light/shading: broad lit/shadow faces, subtle contact darkness at base/join only if not redundant with actual shadows. Do not bake world light into albedo or make wear emissive. Fog and cutaway not part of finish.

## Approved-for-audition effects (proposed, not user-approved)

All amounts are initial art budgets to tune against tactical screenshots, not measured physical predictions. Coordinates are face-local physical units, normalized only for mask sampling. Typical main mark width 4–14 world units (operator reference height 40); 1–2 texel cracks/lines where readable; large paint/repair blocks 8–24. Quantized 2–3 tones per mark. No extra transparent draw per static effect.

Keep stains as cause-specific moisture/use/repair families, not separate generic procedural circles; scrape/scratch/scuff/abrade share contact family; paint-peel/chip one exposed-substrate family. Retire standalone generic radial fade, compulsory random sinusoidal crack, generic unanchored streak, uniform darkening, decorative micro-grain/noise at gameplay distance and randomly scattered wall mud/footprints. No new normal maps, parallax, full-screen filters or geometric dirt. Keep ballistic impacts, blood, smoke, debris, damageable glass/doors and dynamic decals separate and fog-safe; never encode fresh combat marks into static generated site finish.

## Selection and compositing contract

Condition is intensity/likelihood, not one scalar multiplying every mark. Existing condition/era/upkeep plus room role, substrate, coating, exposure and real anchors choose candidates. No new simulation fields or RNG changes to site generation; render-only deterministic seed using segment, face, effect family and anchor ID. Maintain stable placements as slider changes. Candidate events use causeId, owner/face, anchor, effect ID, physical bounds, severity and priority. Deduplicate overlapping marks of same cause; allow adjacent distinct events only within shared budget. Priority: genuine source/repair > material edge/contact > age accent. Compose base motif then finish/fade, exposed substrate/repair, local moisture/residue/contact; preserve material seams, clip to solid surface and avoid crossing apertures. No static effect independently controls alpha or visibility.

Audition defaults per broad wall face: condition 0 clean; 1–25 predominantly clean, at most one small contact mark where valid; 26–50 one or two distinct anchored effects; 51–75 up to three; 76–100 up to four, rare focal severe patch. A face with no valid sources stays mostly clean even at 100. Target untouched face area >=90% lightly worn, >=80% moderate, >=65% heavily neglected (global subtle finish variation excluded); dense localized exceptions only when cause demands. One dominant age/weather event per face, no simultaneous full-surface damp+rust+crack overlay. Coherent room/building contrast; intact clean surfaces remain abundant. Whole-site budget and deterministic selection prevent cost/visual overload on long floorplans. Static effect drawing cost must not scale with number of individual marks after compilation.

## Audition and decision gates

Render actual W1 vs candidate on same site/camera/light; use house, motel, clinic, retail, garage, warehouse, wet room, interior/exterior, window and threshold witnesses; clean 0, 25, 50, 75, 100. Capture tactical and close views, night/flashlight, known/explored/unknown fog and cutaway restored. Show individual effect isolation and combined faces; include intentional empty surfaces and clean controls. Require visible material identity at gameplay zoom and anchored effects with no upside-down runoff; use nearest pixel edges and appropriate mip handling. Compare baked RGB(A) and compact shader mask only if evidence can fairly support both; choose one implementation. No geometry per static mark, no new per-frame generation, no per-wall programs, W1 depth and fog semantics intact. Exact-base measurements and acceptance remain in W2 scope.
