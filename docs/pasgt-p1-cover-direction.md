# PASGT concentrated visual pass — P1/P2

Do not modify PASGT shell volume geometry. This family represents a broad
1980s–1990s full-cut helmet look rather than a manufacturer micro-catalog.

Player-facing choices:
- Shell solid swatch (independent from cover print)
- Cover NO / YES (one fitted geometry; old loose selection resolves to fitted)
- Textile cover pattern (only when cover present)
- Cover construction A/B audition: foliage slits or sewn loops
- Band: none, standard horizontal, crossed
- Concealment: none, foliage, scrim, net

Removed: PASGT quick-presets and inherited shrouds, rails, lights, rear electronics,
NVGs and other modern tactical configuration controls. Existing saved preferences
are retained but unavailable modern parts are suppressed visually.

Art focus: UV continuity on crown and round the four side walls, physical hem,
seam marks in cloth artwork without geometry noise, no vertex-colour AO darkening.
