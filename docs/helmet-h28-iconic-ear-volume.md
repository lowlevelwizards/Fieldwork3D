# H28 — iconic integrated ear volume

## Why H27 was not enough
H27 rendered two upper transitions and a broad ear wall, but the face was
not visually dominant. The leading bevel and two trailing width changes
still read as numerous little wedges. Increasing the width alone would
have enlarged the wedges instead of creating a legible protective volume.

## Geometry contract
- Retain the same crown, chamfer, one continuous shell, and exactly six
  front/ear/nape longitudinal perimeter stations.
- Enlarged continuous ear face: full half-width **5.92** (base 5.075),
  mid half-width **5.78** (base 5.03); high cut stays **5.015**.
- Slightly shorten the front step at the lower side: full ear-start Z -0.46,
  mid Z -0.16, retaining >0.6 station spacing.
- Outer face stays full width at the two central ear stations; the rear
  shoulder returns to the existing nape hem width at the next station.
  This eliminates the second gradual triangular wedge into the rear.
- Unified vertical ear profile: face top ~62% and bottom ~93% toward the
  central ear hem, with one consistent **0.17** upper bevel. Local hem
  clearance is preserved with a single shared clamp, not special leading
  brow conditionals.
- Keep the existing side quad rows for actual volume. Give the upper bevel,
  main protective wall, and underside return matching tonal treatment so
  superficial band shading does not add visual noise.
- Preserve distinct full/mid lower coverage and high cut's ear opening.
- Fitted cover uses exactly the same vertical helper and interpolated width;
  existing binding uses the same six hem points. No separate ear plate,
  accessory or new runtime update.
- Existing flat four-point retention, screws, cloth pattern scale and
  operator customization mappings remain unchanged.

## Acceptance
At mobile Studio and gameplay-fit scales the full/mid guards should read as
one oversized broad panel, with one short leading transition and one clean
rear taper, not repeated fins or a small polygon necklace.
Automated checks enforce widths, face height, rear-taper completion,
reflection symmetry, high-cut cleanliness, cover clearance, static topology,
and intact pre-existing retention/accessory contracts.
Capture bare, plain, woodland and gameplay-fit all-cut WebGL views.
Do not merge until focused/mobile and Ground Zero checks pass, and verify
published Pages returns the exact stamped release build.
