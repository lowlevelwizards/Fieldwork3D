# H27 — broad lower-ear profiles and flat nylon webbing

H26 put the ear crest at the right vertical onset but still formed a
triangular ridge. The top and lower crest now share the same X width:
- full-cut side half-width 5.67, mid-cut 5.56, high-cut flat 5.015
- start from a quiet upper side wall; short outward bevel at .52–.66
- hold width from fraction .66 through .90 for a broad protective face
- tuck inward over .90–1.00 to the existing lower hem
- preserve six longitudinal stations, brow/ear/temple Z transitions, rear
  nape, flat crown, chunky chamfer and existing low-poly materials

Full/mid are a single triangulated shell, no added side plates. The high cut
has no lateral ear bulge; it retains its open-ear contour and rear nape.
Corresponding cover sampling rings track the actual shell face top and
bottom, same print atlas, same inward cloth hem and hidden bare rubber lip.

H26 forced three independent angled retention ribbons to share one rotated
edge at the cheek. H27 instead builds each straight span as a true planar
constant-width quad (2 triangles, 0.78 units wide), with outward-facing
side straps and front-facing chinward straps. No needle-point taper,
triangular distortion, overlapping caps, or new dynamic systems. Retain
four mounting legs, straps tucked beneath shell, chin cup and hardware.
