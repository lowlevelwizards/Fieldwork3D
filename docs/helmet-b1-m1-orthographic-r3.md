# B1 R3 — US M1 Orthographic Identity
## Why the previous build still looked generic
Mobile Studio feedback established that the eight-sided model was finally stylistically aligned with the game, but remained too shallow, too wide, symmetrical front-to-back, and lacked a readable underside.
## Real-world construction references
- American Militaria Reference: nominal M1 shell dimensions 7 in tall, 9.5 in wide, 11 in long; longer than wide; modest front lip.
- US Army Medical Department WWII historical survey: two-piece Hadfield steel shell and separate liner, increased side/rear coverage.
- Original period M1 examples (International Military Antiques) and front/side/back collectors' views: distinct forward projecting visor/peak and rear bowl dropping toward nape.
These are proportional references, not instructions to mechanically scale the cuboid head model.
## Locked art direction
- Keep just eight perimeter vertices and one major crown shoulder.
- Height increase: roof front approximately 10.24 world-local Y vs 8.98 prior.
- Width reduction: rim 12.10 total vs 13.76, but preserve exaggeration relative to head.
- Front/back extent: 6.93 forward / 6.55 rear (ratio depth-to-width ~1.11).
- M1 beak projects 1.07 past lower front wall; rear projection only .35.
- Rim plane rises toward the face and falls toward the nape: approximately .64-.68 Y difference.
- Upper crown shifts rearward .38, tapering down to .08 at lower wall.
- A recessed inner wall returns upward from the rolled edge, visible at low camera angle. No solid floor over the head.
- Fitted/loose covers, retaining band, net and retention anchors must use the same sloped profile; no separate geometric fallback.
- 104 shell triangles, still ~50% below initial 210-triangle M1.
## Acceptance
Front, right side, back, and low fit view, plus colorless comparisons to approved ACH/MICH. Check that the M1 is recognizable by the beak, swept rear, taller bowl, and dark underside without extra details. No impact on historical/equipment saves, other helmets, gameplay geometry, or production until review.
