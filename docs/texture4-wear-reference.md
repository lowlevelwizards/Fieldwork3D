Visual checks: clinic 120, garage 126, warehouse 127, restaurant 128,
apartment 134, utility 141 and office 158.

comparisons with the PR or its CI artifacts. Use `wearStats()` and

full-surface grime pass. Collision and ballistic material IDs remain untouched.
