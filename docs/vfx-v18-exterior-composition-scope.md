## V18.3 — Archetype programs and art
Use existing library first, audit each silhouette at gameplay distance; chunky/stubby/iconic, rectangles and broad planes before bevels, texture lines instead of geometry, actual functional dimensions, no tiny unseen detail.
- House/townhouse: mailbox and address at real frontage, porch/bench, bins by side path, grill/picnic seat at rear, narrow trunk trees, shed if space, boundary fence with real gate/open gap; avoid commercial street furniture in private yard.
- Apartment: common entry bench, collective refuse/recycling, bike parking, shared courtyard seating and utilities, separate public vs resident areas.
- Motel: marked bay row and walk to actual rooms, ice/vending at appropriate access, benches, refuse at service edge; no fake vehicle drive lanes.
- Clinic: accessible arrival markings, wayfinding, bench and bollards where legal; medical deliveries and screened refuse at service area, clear entry.
- Retail: sign/entry approach, cart return or racks, bollards, delivery pallets/totes and refuse behind; no stock crates across customer lane.
- Restaurant: paired tables/seating in actual patio, signage/awning aligned to facade, service bins and delivery equipment separate from guests. Compact seating asset/cluster review if existing dimensions cannot fit.
- Office: entry sign, bench/bike parking, shared utility/refuse, usable parking if area allows.
- Garage: open bay apron, tire stack, tool chest, compressor/parts storage, safety bollards near service edge; preserve bay traffic.
- Warehouse: loading staging, pallets/crates, container only in genuinely large area, screened refuse, utility and perimeter; keep central truck/worker access open.
- Utility: cabinet, service equipment, cable reel, access hatch and functional security boundary; never cover a required maintenance route.
- Other supported subtypes: explicitly map to closest functional program, don't silently default to random residential scatter.
Vehicles are explicitly OUT OF SCOPE by user decision. Preserve all retired vehicle exclusions and historical regression assertions. Do not author new cars, vans, pickups, vehicle models, placeholders or driveable mechanics. Keep parking/service approach geometry and improve its markings, furniture and functional use without vehicles. A separate future vehicle overhaul will own model art, scale, parking occupancy and physical behavior.
Boundaries: reuse existing fence/gate/half-wall assets; create meaningful perimeter breaks only as actual physical gaps/doors, never a decorative obstructing object. No continuous full perimeter by default; preserve alternate approach choices. No grass/bush reinstatement; retain trees and flat lawn, gravel, soil; audit planter foliage as existing asset, prefer empty/low-maintenance alternatives if art conflicts with retirement.
