# R6 — Engagement Space Exposure Tuning

Audit basis: R5's 11-seed mission survey reported 72 geometrically
overexposed rooms. This is **all room types**, including small utility,
service and sanitary spaces where complex cover is not reasonable.

R6 does not replace rooms or invent cover.

- Retain the original threshold-based sightline measurement.
- Report both raw overexposure across every room and a separately filtered
  meaningful-engagement-room exposure count.
- For optional existing tall fixtures in sufficiently large engagement rooms,
  compare at most five already-legal candidate placements. Prefer a mix of
  sightlines and concealment while preserving at least two exposed standing
  samples and enough walkable sampling locations.
- Mandatory fixtures, low cover, door/circulation reservations, physical
  collision and room completeness are unchanged.
- Run the focused tests and 11-seed audited passive simulation before
  considering any release; use an additional real gameplay pass to judge
  whether furniture feels natural.

Do not interpret the refined overexposure count as a direct before/after
reduction in room geometry without also comparing the raw metric. The metric
classification changed intentionally, while spatial placement behavior may
also change.

## First 11-seed findings

- Baseline R5: **72 raw overexposed rooms** (all purposes).
- R6: **72 raw overexposed rooms** in the same deterministic sample;
  **42** belong to larger engagement-relevant spaces.
- No invalid site or replay chronology results.
- Furniture-choice ranking did not yet reduce the *binary* raw
  overexposure count. This is a diagnostic refinement and a conservative
  placement pass, not a demonstrated tactical-quality breakthrough.
- The survey now groups engagement exposure by room purpose to identify
  defensible next changes based on specific functional spaces rather than
  scattering arbitrary obstacles through every open area.
