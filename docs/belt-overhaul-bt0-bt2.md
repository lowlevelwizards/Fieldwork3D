# BT0–BT2 belt overhaul

Four representative styles plus none. Existing light and standard equipment IDs are retained as leather and utility; old light-belt appearance maps to leather. Legacy standard kits can retain historical battle/padded appearances without silently upgrading their owned equipment. Explicit new selection equips the distinct item. Proposed capacity 1/4/5/6 is magazine-equivalent, not a second inventory. No item issuance or new gameplay capability. Historical waist rigs remain separately owned until cross-carrier fitting.

BT1 replaces the placeholder with silhouette-specific geometry. BT2 defines a shared pelvis-local contact frame and deterministic footprint reservation contract. Actual equipment placement, holder artwork, retrieval and cross-carrier integration are separate later stages.
