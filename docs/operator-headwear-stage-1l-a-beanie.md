# Stage 1L-A — Beanie: two-mass silhouette

## Why
Stage 1K fixed the perched fit but left three apparent perimeter tiers: a narrower upper-crown lid, a wider body, and a still wider lower cuff. The Stage 1L art-direction limit is **two visible reads** only: `crown + folded cuff`.

## Geometry change
- **Crown:** one continuous flat upper plane joined to four head-fitting sides at precisely the same outside width (X±4.60) and front/back (Z±4.70). No incidental crown shoulder or trim. Top Y7.92; fitted sidewalls descend to Y4.58.
- **Folded cuff:** one visually continuous lower band built from four intersecting ring sectors. It wraps upper forehead/ear, with bottom Y3.77 and exterior X±4.90 / Z±5.00. This adds exactly **one** purposeful 0.30-unit outward transition.
- The main head cavity stays open below the crown: no solid box across the face or within the skull. One cuboid-union exterior mesh, identical canonical attachment, no new material.

The model still uses nine source volumes because an open lower head fitting needs separate front, rear and side sectors. **These are not nine visible design masses.** Two visible levels are formally checked by the focused tests across multiple Y slices.

## Protected scope
- Existing `ach` and `high-cut` arrays/metrics untouched.
- Patrol cap, boonie, PASGT and Soviet steel geometries untouched; scheduled for Stage 1L-B onward.
- No changes to rig, animation, inventory, damage, or materials.

## Visual approval checklist
Front/side/3/4 and standard gameplay zoom:
- Recognizable knit cap with one thick fold.
- Crown and body have one continuous outer wall, no horizontal shelf near the top.
- Cuff genuinely surrounds head at brow/upper-ear height.
- No mushroom silhouette, wide protective helmet shell, or exposed hovering gap.
- No visible little narrow crown steps. Technical union seams must not make new exterior ledges.

## Release
Focused headgear tests, existing ACH/high-cut protections, and production typecheck/build. Merge only on green gate; confirm Pages' build stamp before starting Stage 1L-B.
