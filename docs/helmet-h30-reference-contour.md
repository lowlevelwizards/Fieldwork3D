## What the references establish
- Forehead edge is nearly horizontal and sits above the eyes.
- A **full cut** descends around the ear; the protection is part of the
  shell's lower contour, not a rectangular ear block glued to the side.
- **Mid cut** raises the ear region; **high cut** leaves it open.
- The nape remains covered, independently of the ear cut.
- Side shell forms one broadly continuous mass, rounded in reality and
  translated to a few intentional broad angular planes in Fieldwork.

## What this prototype changes relative to H28 and H29
- Keep crown, squared top chamfer and all six approved lower YZ stations.
- Modest lateral sidewall swelling: full +0.320, mid +0.305, high +0.000.
  H28's +0.845/+0.750 looked like an added box; H29's near-zero shaping
  looked like a deep featureless slab.
- The small upper bevel starts at brow +0.35 and ends at brow +0.17.
  A broad side plane continues down to each station's hem +0.14, with a
  final short protective return to the existing lower contour.
- Ear silhouette, not lateral bulge, supplies the coverage.
- Keep existing four-point straps, rubber binding, covers, atlas sampling,
  accessories, and single static shell mesh.

## Explicit hold criteria
- Screenshot **before and after** from fixed front, strict side, both
  three-quarter angles, and rear for each full/mid/high cut.
- Judge side silhouette and brow-to-ear landmarks; do not approve on CI
  alone. Reject narrow triangles, a projecting add-on box, a flat slab,
  exposed cover lip, unnatural rear return, or a broken high-cut arch.
- Keep PR draft; do not merge to main or deploy until user approval
  of the specific visual candidate. CI is necessary but not sufficient.
