# Tactical follow, throw, contact and notice pass

## Behavior
- An active followed civilian or surrendered suspect faces their actual
  assigned operator; the follower goal may side-step into a *behind/outboard*
  pocket when in the lead's near-forward travel corridor. Their existing
  swept movement and door authority remain unchanged.
- Selected authored suspects, civilians, patients, weapons and movable props
  receive a white dot in either map view; fog-disclosed objects only.
- OPEN while paused previews a real pre-open contact anchor plus high-ready
  if the current operator can reach the door. No remote-hand teleport.
- FLASH uses direct straight airborne flight, one valid wall reflection, or
  a reachable physical corner-hand release. The hand reach leg is not a
  midair flight segment. Fog does not hide the flash cue, but does hide people.
- Historical interrupted door and resolved blocked order state are not
  presented as new live warnings; motor history still exists for diagnostics.

## Guardrails / follow-up checks
- Inspect corner-adjacent actual geometry on phone, including near-closed
  door scenarios and a distant frame that must not become a valid hook.
- Stress moving escort through narrow motel/clinic rooms, particularly a
  follower that begins *ahead* of the operator, with a stationary civilian
  beside a jammed door.
- Verify hand/wrist and high-ready on an adjacent OPEN with both plan-only
  previews and actual GO interaction.
- Do not turn off collision, reveal an unseen hostile, or add a secondary
  navigation/motor authority to make these gestures appear successful.
