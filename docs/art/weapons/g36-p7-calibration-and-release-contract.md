# G36 P7 — calibrated iconic proportions (P6-protective release)

Status: **feature branch / not user art approved**. P6 commit `8cea6fb` is the last known accepted **direction**, not a claim of final approval. P7 must earn its changes in the actual production builder.

## Strict scope

1. **P7.0** Lock the P6 original full-size dual-optic G36 as a reference. Compare with actual Arsenal M16A2, FNC service, FAL 50.00, AKM and G3A3 using the existing G36 seven-weapon WebGL lab and the shared, equal-scale camera/lighting policy.
2. **P7.1** Evaluate two *non-stacked accessory* optic arrangements: P6 original, a lower magnified-body emphasis, and a rear-biased upper reflex. Preserve total optic height (Y 4.70), handle roof/open space, primary sight positions and actual factory behavior. Pick only one if its geometry and rendered silhouette improve integration.
3. **P7.2** Test magazine curve alone (not length and width together). Preserve the P6 3.32 body width, 5.30 exposed art length, 1.83 depth, mouth position and original unique G36 feed. Keep the polymer facet and avoid transparency/ribs/new meshes.
4. **P7.3** Do **not** touch receiver, handguard, stock, grip, barrel or muzzle unless equal-scale P7 comparison exposes a concrete deficiency. Existing P6 side/front/top proportions stay the reference.
5. **P7.4** Prove production master is the unchanged six-slot factory with actual mounting/reload/hand/sight/muzzle contacts; verify physical stock/handle/trigger openings, real polygon/mesh economy, browser side/quarter/opposite/top/front/clay/flat 64/128 px and peers.
6. **P7.5** Commit independently, merge as one release batch only after checks and verification of previous deployed Pages build. Never queue a deployment behind one in progress. User mobile acceptance remains a separate gate.

## Art guardrails

- Less mechanical literalness, **no** decorative chamfers, receiver ports, extra vent graphics or micro detail.
- No uniform enlargement; optic **relationship** is more important than a prescribed 15–25% size increase.
- No change to carry-handle span, two stock openings, buttpad, grip and planar hard-normal three-station handguard.
- Keep `g36-service` and all saved six-part IDs, G36-only magazine feed, factory integrated-sight/mounting gating, reload/mag/hand/muzzle/sight contracts, appearance catalog and material allocation behavior.
- One-variable comparison: upper-optic placement is separated from lower-optic shape; magazine sweep separated from width and length.
- **No-change is a valid result** if an experiment does not read better than P6 in matched production comparisons.

## Release decision record (to fill from actual tests)

- **P7.1 production selection:** rear-integrated upper reflex. It translates the short
  crown about half an art unit rearward within the existing integrated carrying
  handle, but does not increase optic height, move the lower optic, hide the
  long handle opening, or add mesh details. The alternative larger lower scope
  was not selected: it deepens the rear mass and sacrifices valuable opening.
  Matching WebGL comparison captures remain an art-review aid, **not proof of
  user approval**.
- **P7.2 production selection:** no change from P6 magazine. The sweep
  candidate retained the same centerline length but changed the absolute
  lower corner envelope through tangent-normal geometry. It is available
  for comparison only; without affirmative visual evidence, retaining P6
  protects the silhouette/contact assumptions. No claim that this candidate
  is historically inferior or visually rejected by the user.
- **P7.3 structural changes:** none. P6 receiver, stock, buttpad, planar
  handguard, grip, barrel and muzzle remain untouched.
- **P7.4:** critical browser/geometry/build validation required on final commit.
  Human on-device aesthetic approval remains pending.
- P6 stays available in review tooling without creating any saved weapon variants.
