# G36 V13 — reference-first stock replacement

**2026-11-17.** User-selected stylized V13 silhouette, derived from the side-on G36 polymer folding-stock photograph provided in the conversation. The image is a *shape reference*, not fabrication geometry.

## Scope

Replace the existing P8 stock profile in the **same live** `buildG36OriginalBlockout` production builder. This is a stock-only art change; no new factory preset, part IDs, folding animation, accessories, inventory, ballistics, or sight/hand/muzzle positions. No standalone preview mesh substitutes for the production model.

## Approved silhouette

- One uninterrupted chunky molded main frame and exactly two physical openings.
- Tall rear butt/heel; lower sweep climbs toward the hinge through a few deliberate faceted turns.
- Large high rear opening with a rising lower boundary and smaller forward rectangular aperture.
- A shallow upper spine with raised forward shoulder, no decorative grooves, screws, or rails.
- Keep existing restrained 1.72-unit depth, slim buttpad, hinge connection, and stock rear/muzzle anchors.

## Source coordinate transfer

The supplied standalone photo showed hinge at left, butt at right. V13 mockup was mirrored to the in-game side presentation, then transferred into game-local XY (+X muzzle, +Y up) and fitted between the existing stock/receiver anchor extents. The rear upper corner is extended minimally into the existing buttpad to prevent a visible gap. The profile has **12 outside vertices, 5 large-opening vertices, and 4 small-opening vertices**.

## Acceptance

- Legacy G36 physical-hole ray samples remain correct on both openings and on the solid heel.
- Stock is still a member of the original six saved body slots; the main builder and factory materials are unchanged.
- Stock main silhouette, pad, and hinge intersect in 3D; overall endpoints remain tied to `G36_B_ART`.
- No extra meshes or textures were introduced. Review the real Workbench/Arsenal mobile view after release; screenshots/CI do not replace human aesthetic approval.
