# R870 P16 — compact pistol-grip silhouette corrective pass
## Screenshot witness
November 19 mobile Workbench screenshots of MCS Breacher and CQB showed an exaggerated diagonal polymer wedge beneath the receiver. On CQB, the original skeleton's lower beam crossed the same visual region, reading as a second grip. The wood-stock R870 and front hardware were approved; do not rebuild them.

## Narrow fix
- Use one 12-point, front-seated, deeper vertical pistol grip for both rear variants. Its lower faceted heel sits at x −9.54/y −5.19 vs the P12 old x −10.96/y −4.76. This is a **graphic contour**, not a firearm specification.
- Retain receiver join points x −6.12 at y +.76/−1.76 and unchanged pistol firing-hand target (−8.45,−3.10), which still lies within the grip.
- Give the MCS shoulder variant exactly the same 2.60-depth grip as the pistol-only version; previously it inherited the 2.90 non-pistol default.
- Keep the skeletal rail and buttplate and all three stock meshes. Land the lower diagonal at the rear grip socket rather than drawing it across the long handle. Keep real stockX −14.40.
- Update only stockless pistol-only rear datum to x −9.54 so model and shoulderless assembly agree. No ballistics, class, capacity, reload, inventory, front-end, wood, SGA, birdshead, grip IDs or campaign changes.

## Gates
Focused P12–P16 R870/Arsenal geometry and pose tests, TypeScript/production build, and actual WebGL desktop/phone review before release. If comparison screenshots are unavailable, hold as draft until assessed rather than assuming a passing geometry test proves visual quality.
