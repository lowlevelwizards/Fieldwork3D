# G36 H1 — essential original handguard forms and quiet graphic vents

**Target:** `HK G36 · Original Dual Optic` production factory, as supplied in user's November 17 2026 Arsenal phone screenshots, compared against the previously supplied original G36 rifle references and the real FNC/HK33 Arsenal geometry builders.

**Approval:** user requested the six-corner H1 handguard rebuild, retaining its original factory footprint, then simpler elongated vents. Aesthetic acceptance of the live implementation is reserved for user's subsequent on-device review, consistent with the universal production standard.

## Locked identity and comparison

- G36 **original long service handguard**, not G36C/G36K, aftermarket rail, G3 steel tube, or FNC gas-block composition.
- **FNC** uses a six-corner sculpted polymer fore-end plus distinct gas upper; H1 borrows the *large-plane economy*, not the gas block or proportions.
- **HK33** has an eight-facet slim/tubular forearm; H1 remains wider, flatter and heavier.
- **FAL/AK/AR** remain visually different: no wood or stamped receiver extension, no triangular AR furniture, no rail/handstop details.
- Existing rear half (V13 stock and O3 dual optic), receiver, grip, mag, barrel, scale and finish palette stay unchanged.

## Stage 1: true six-corner faceted shell

Replace the legacy four-corner rectangle in `g36-handguard-profiles.ts` with the following six-point section. At each of the unchanged three X stations:

1. Flat roof at `roofY` with half-width `0.76 × halfDepth`.
2. One large sloping shoulder dropping `15.5% of section height` to full `halfDepth`.
3. One broad almost-vertical side plane down to the floor, which tucks in to `0.84 × halfDepth`.
4. Flat substantial floor (no pointed keel), mirrored on both sides.

Keep the existing station datums exactly:

| X | Roof Y | Floor Y | Maximum half-depth |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3.75 (receiver contact) | +1.35 | -1.88 | 1.45 |
| 11.75 (long constant-width body) | +1.35 | -1.88 | 1.45 |
| 13.45 (short blunt nose) | +0.96 | -1.64 | 1.32 |

No extra station, collar, vent hole mesh, rail, secondary polymer strip, bevel or cylinder. A single `family-handguard` mesh carries all factory slots as before. New handguard is 32 triangles vs 20 for the rectangular legacy shape; entire G36 remains under the existing 600-triangle/22-mesh regression ceilings. Use flat normals rather than pillow smoothing. Retain current factory side/collision, animation and hand-contact landmarks.

## Stage 2: exactly four slender side slots

- Same `g36PixelAtlas()` pooled 384×192 atlas (no other texture, shader, or material).
- Four existing centers on both sides at `X=5.35, 7.40, 9.45, 11.50`.
- Each painted aperture: `1.70` units long in X (`center ± .85`), but only `0.56` units tall (Y `-0.17 .. 0.39`). The former height was `0.94`, so this is 40% shallower.
- In appearance these are **graphic shaded vents**, not fake physically punched openings or added vent meshes. Dark ink remains 89 with normal material/lighting interaction; no lip, frame, screws or micro-stripes.
- Preserve right-side-only ejection port, smoked G36 magazine panels, and blank lower receiver.

- Physical external profile with six unique XY/Z landmarks at each station, flat roof and floor; shoulder broadens while floor narrows, dimensional width and length remain fixed.
- G36 receiver/handguard/barrel physical contact, magazine/muzzle/rear stock endpoints, weapon part IDs and O3 optic unchanged.

- Compare actual FNC and HK33 production models at the same view. Tests show construction, but **aesthetic approval remains on-device**.
