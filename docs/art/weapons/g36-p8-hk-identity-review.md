# G36 P8 — HK identity and miniature production review

Art-only iteration of the existing live original full-length G36. References are the supplied original dual-optic G36, handguard, receiver-side, optic and polymer-stock photos (November 17 review), compared against current production M16A2, FNC, FAL, AKM, G3 and HK33. These images guide **visible shape relationships**, not engineering or mechanical functionality.

## Intentional changes (do not add detail beyond these)
- **Stock**: continuous broad descending/sweeping two-segment lower silhouette, preserved two true openings; slim rear pad and compact shared-stock hinge.
- **Receiver / magwell**: one more visibly raked front cheek, preserved magazine insertion center and no trigger-guard frame; no cosmetic construction panel strips.
- **Grip**: decisive rearward rake, preserved primary-hand datum within the body and traditional short chunky HK feel.
- **Integrated dual optic**: one 7-corner main housing with one upper reflex crown and two large face cues, no rails or micro collars; historical P6/P7 lab review modes retained.
- **HK selector**: reuse the same eight-sided hub and chunky paddle helper as existing HK weapons; one pair per side, no tiny letters or free-floating decals.
- **Charging tab**: one substantial low box meeting the receiver behind the handle opening; not an animated weapon manipulation mechanic.
- **Ejection port**: ONE dark side-correct graphic cutout on the existing upper shell; no extra mesh, no mirrored opposite-side opening.
- **Fore-end**: unchanged flat four-sided bulk with stronger single sloping front roof and four high-contrast oversized vents; no chamfer ladder.
- **Magazine**: same curved body and unique G36 feed, same mouth and rig/reload contacts; restrained dark smoky-polymer color and two broad body value planes, no individual rounds, ribs, real transparency or sorting costs.
- **Muzzle**: slightly stouter single terminal form, unchanged tip X and original barrel.
- **Junctions**: retain purposeful real receiver/guard and stock/receiver intersections; avoid hidden connector overlays.

## Stop/acceptance tests
- Exact six saved body part IDs, one factory identity and unchanged original dual optics/mount gating, ammunition, inventory, reload and shot results.
- Physical through-holes in stock and carry bridge, true trigger gap; selector/charging do not obstruct these.
- Actual side, 3/4, opposite, top/front at matching scale against peer builders, 64/128 px Workbench, operator and reload visual test.
- Art review roughly 20–28 meshes and <=600 triangles is a **review trigger**, not a hard stylistic dictate. Count from actual live builder. Cached atlas must be side-correct, one upload, unchanged mesh count.
