## Purpose
Establish graphic miniature realism: confident primary forms, compact character, broad purposeful facets, readable negative spaces, graphic surface rhythm and tactile materials. The strongest existing FAL/FNC/G36/870/Glock artwork is a benchmark candidate, not an exemption. Authentic identity rather than literal replication. All assessments remain provisional until standardized captures are reviewed.

## WA2 — Universal Weapon Art Standard 2.0
- WA2.1 Review baseline contact sheets against benchmark candidates and identify shared signature traits, counterexamples and intentional exceptions. Approve a small cross-family calibration set (FAL, FNC, G36, AR, AK, MP5, 870, Glock, M9), not a single family as universal scale target.
- WA2.2 Form rules: silhouette first; distinguish dominant/subordinate masses; rectangles by default; broad visible facets, no micro bevels, purposeful openings, integrated volumes and proportions appropriate to the family. Strong silhouette at native size takes priority over micro-detail. No indiscriminate thickening or uniform scaling; long-gun vs pistol guard convention remains an art decision. Use existing art-only landmarks and preserve presentation invariants.
- WA2.3 Graphic surface grammar: classify primary form, secondary identity, tertiary graphic and removable micro detail. Match projected line weight and contrast; favor baked/cached atlas for shallow marks, geometry for meaningful outline or negative space; avoid decorative overlays or z-fighting. Preserve intentional asymmetry. Compare at 64/128px and operator scale; do not invent pixel results without renders.
- WA2.4 Material grammar: common semantic substrates (steel, polymer, wood, resin, rubber, lenses, painted finish), broad value separation, controlled saturation/roughness and visible matte facets. Preserve authentic variant colors; no global uniform green-gray wash. Weathering is a separate WA14 treatment, not default dirt. Define illustrative swatches pending actual calibrated game lighting.
- WA2.5 Accessory and variant language: coherent shape/line weights across existing library; distinct identity not gratuitous detail; valid configurations only. Keep preview/issued status and saved IDs. Separate art decisions from gameplay, compatibility or historical exactness.
- WA2.6 Publish Standard 2.0 with hierarchy, annotated actual examples, before/after study evidence where available, family-specific exception register, review checklist and pipeline. Existing standard 1.0 remains authoritative until 2.0 is reviewed and adopted; preserve technical invariants. All future WA work references this contract.
- WA2 exit: every rule has a rationale, evidence or explicitly provisional status, measurable/visual verification method and exceptions where appropriate. No blanket visual approval until actual review. No remodelling in WA0–WA2.

## Invariants and release gate
No weapon gameplay, physics, ammo, capacity, stats, timing, recipes, saves, attachment compatibility, inventory, faction access, operator pose/contact or canonical art change. No new weapon families. No real-world functional/internal designs. Keep existing production paths and shared ownership intact. Use targeted inventory/appearance/geometry tests, typecheck, build and real WebGL smoke; do not run redundant broad suites. Stage commits on feature branch. Draft PR until evidence complete. Group release after critical checks and previous production Pages deployment verified successful; do not pile merges. Explicitly distinguish source-level findings, generated artifacts, measured outcomes and human visual review. Do not mark incomplete stages complete.
