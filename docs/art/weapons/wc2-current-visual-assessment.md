## Overall
The major silhouettes remain readable, but the visual language varies from deliberate carved masses to literal flat wedges and assembled primitives. Simplification by deletion is not equivalent to authored caricature. The greatest opportunity is sculptural intent: every feature should strengthen identity and rhythm, not simply meet a mesh budget. Material revisions alone cannot resolve the weak forms. The visible stocks are the most obvious cross-family example.
