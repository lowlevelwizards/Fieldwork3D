# G36 P3 — rear-heel stock and integrated optic refinement

The P3 art pass rebuilds the **same six-slot** original service G36, retaining the signed factory magazine/optic contacts and 34.55-unit overall length.

- Rear stock: lower outside heel descends from -4.43 to -5.24; enlarged lower contour, newly descending rear diagonal, two actual negative-space openings, reduced depth to 1.78. Buttpad follows the heel while avoiding a heavier three-quarter slab.
- Carry handle: slim front inner support and depth without closing its true void; keep front-sided interior walls intact.
- Original dual optic: an inset dark rear eyepiece on the long housing, a genuinely protruding dark upper-reflex frontal window, and the existing low-facet primary optic face. No rails, screws, micro detailing, additional accessories or gameplay optics logic.
- Barrel: enlarge the simple square cross-section from 0.76 to 1.14; muzzle stays a 1.00-square blunt termination. Both remain exactly 12 triangles.
- Receiver: soften the overlarge lower magwell slab into one steeper undercut, preserve grip and magazine contacts without a trigger guard.
- Magazine: preserve shared STANAG *appearance* and separate G36-only feed/part. Darker smoked polymer, subtler three atlas ribs, no geometry layers.
- Workbench: factory modified-state compares accessories against the selected factory preset rather than the class's default iron sights, resolving G36's false 'Modified' label without hiding real edits.

**Acceptance**: Run G36 geometry + P3 tests, Arsenal catalog/finish/workbench regression, all-factory geometry census (P3 <=430 tris, <=16 meshes), FNC/FAL nonregression, TypeScript/build and real-browser G36 visual checks. The final aesthetic judgment belongs to in-device user screenshots.
