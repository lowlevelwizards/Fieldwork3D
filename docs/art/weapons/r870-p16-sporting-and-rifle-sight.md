# R870 P16 — sporting and rifle-sighted batch

**Art lock:** Preserve the approved P13 wood stock/grip and P14/P15 tactical/compact variants. Use shared six-slot Arsenal and mission renderer; no new weapon class or made-up ballistics.

## Stage P16.A — Sporting 26-inch
- One extra-long 26-inch **Fieldwork-stylized** barrel profile, muzzleX=26.88. Same standard six-shell fixed tube; no fake new capacity.
- A distinct, slim long wooden pump (endX=12.50) with clean uninterrupted wood faces and no decoratively engraved groove meshes.
- Raised ventilated rib drawn as one long simple bar and three supporting posts. This changes the silhouette clearly, not dozens of subpixel vents.
- New factory reference: Field 26-inch Style.
- Use the same selected barrel extent for visual barrel, shot clearance and attachment mount. Reflect added forward mass and yaw inertia in assembly handling.

## Stage P16.B — Rifle-Sighted 20-inch
- Separate muzzleX=21.12, same standard tube and classic Police wood/black pump choices, distinct oversized front blade and rear sight, no optic rail mesh.
- New factory reference: Rifle-Sighted 20-inch Style with black synthetic furniture.
- Do not claim ballistics accuracy, new slug damage, or ammunition types: this stage is a distinct sight/barrel configuration first. Specialized slug mechanics are a later balanced pass.

## Compatibility
- 10in: mini pump + 3-round mini tube, 14in: compact polymer pump + 4-round short tube, 18in: standard/extended tube with normal tactical/wood pumps, 20in: standard tube with Police wood/black pump, 26in: standard tube + long sporting wood pump.
- Any Barrel/Tube/Pump change uses atomic companion proposals and tolerant legacy-save normalization. Never erase owned rounds.
