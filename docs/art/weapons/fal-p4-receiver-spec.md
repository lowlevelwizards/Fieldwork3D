# FN FAL P4 — Receiver-only stylization
## Art target
Chunky/expressive Fieldwork miniature: one crowned roof over broader lower
shoulder, strongly stepped lower belly/magwell, right high scalloped ejection
shape, left large slide/handle, exactly one oversize pivot boss per side, one
genuinely open trigger-guard silhouette. No fine hardware or extra furniture.
Real FN patterns vary; simplified features are iconic visual cues, not functional
mechanical replicas. Do NOT copy the raised aftermarket Picatinny receiver rail.

## Stages
- R1A: 6 longitudinal stations, eight faceted YZ points each, crown top Z 1.16
  versus max side Z 1.69; existing -6.60 to +3.85 and roof Y1.83 retained
- R1B: lower 12-point profile with proud 3.40 depth, sloped well still ends
  Y=-3.19; existing 20-round magazine, insertion and handguard unchanged
- R2A: right raised high eight-corner scoop conforming to crowned upper
  surface, left one-piece forward handle and long track, two 8-sided pivot discs
- R2B: open negative-space seven-sided guard and narrow 0.38-depth frame,
  fully part of receiver geometry and never simulated as a trigger
- R3: old and new focused tests, true THREE.js side, opposite side, 3/4 and
  mobile screenshots versus M4A1 / G3A3 at identical scale

## Invariants and checks
- No added weapon or receiver part IDs; maintain six FAL component identities
- Existing stockX=-15, muzzleX=22, magazine top Y=-2.98 and carry anchors
- No behavior, sound, reload, ballistics, animations or other family changes
- One-time construction only, bounded exterior mesh count 25 (P3 was 21)
- Artifact screenshots are **proof of rendering**, not automatic artistic approval.
  Inspect actual mobile-scale silhouette and shaded receiver in Arsenal.
