## Reference decisions

- PASGT: original US PASGT, whose front rim sits around the eyebrow, and ear coverage extends lower than ACH. See [U.S. Army fit comparison](https://safety.army.mil/Portals/0/Documents/MEDIA/MEDIAARCHIVE/MAGAZINES/COUNTERMEASURE/Standard/CM2005.pdf) and original PASGT side photographs.
- SSh-40: Soviet SSh-40, rather than later conical SSh-68 or an M1/Stahlhelm/kettle. See [original SSh-40 front/side/rear record](https://relicsandrifles.com/fieldgear/russia/ssh40.html) and [SSh family comparison](https://internationalmilitaria.wordpress.com/2021/01/11/guide-on-identifying-soviet-russian-ssh40-ssh60-and-ssh68/).

## Regressions and visual approval

Focused tests check actual source volumes, head openings, widths at vertical slices, PASGT vs unchanged ACH coverage, minimal SSh-40 flare, and unchanged ACH/high-cut regression targets. Normal gameplay, front, side and 3/4 visual approval is still reserved for user inspection. Do not add rails, straps, seams, textures, rivets or material detail until approved.
