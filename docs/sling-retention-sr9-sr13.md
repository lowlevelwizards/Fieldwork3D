# SR9–13 — Anatomy, transition, motion and visual identities
- Retention uses the unified anatomical fallback and continuous fitted sleeve strategy. Released elbows move toward the neutral mannequin pole; hands use stance- and stride-dependent targets. Dedicated action and pistol hand owners are not replaced.
- Fixed-step transition fraction still owns gameplay/replay. Earlier hand release, continuous drop/side transfer, and a progressing webbing route replace the generic presentation. No new timer or custody state.
- Suspension uses existing movement phase, mass/inertia, posture and recorded decaying drive/brake impulses. Translational motion and bounded outward excursions complement the rotations. No render-time physics or cloth simulation.
- Three art profiles specify width, color, body return height and motion freedom while preserving single/two-point capability classes and saved equipment IDs.
- Limitations: numerical tests do not establish visual acceptance. Arm/gear clearance remains approximate, back transfer webbing is a sampled exterior arc, motion damping is an analytic envelope not a physical spring. Interaction/transport and Lab diagnostics follow SR14–19.
