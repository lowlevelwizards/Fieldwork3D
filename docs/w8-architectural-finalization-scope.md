## Purpose and non-negotiable boundaries
Finish W0–W7 as one coherent visual and resource pipeline, with recognizable real-world interiors, calm broad shapes, readable material identity, and measurable combined efficiency. Chunky, stubby, iconic, simplified geometry; authentic with least geometry; graphic pixel-scale detail instead of micro-bevels or ornamental meshes. Architecture should frame people and tactical information, not compete with them. Clean is not sterile everywhere; neglected is not uniformly brown. No new effect solely to fill scope. No changes to generated physical map, collision, paths, ballistics, doors, cover, AI, mission semantics, evidence authority, fog authorization or archived replay. V18 owns exterior density; vehicles and new prop catalogs separate; dynamic blood, impacts, glass, dust, smoke, muzzle/detonation, damage remain independently owned. No new render targets or per-frame CPU floor/wall wear generation.
