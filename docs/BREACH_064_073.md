## Architecture

The existing fixed-step simulation in `breach-game.ts` remains **the only
gameplay authority**: navigation, doors, sound, vision, AI, shots, objectives
and mission outcome.

`breach-3d.ts` is only a *read-only renderer*. World units are transformed
consistently: old tactical point `(x,y)` becomes 3D `(x,height,y)`.
No new 3D pathfinding or AI has been invented.

`breach-main.ts` handles input and presentation. A Three.js ray cast onto
the ground plane gives the exact world point under a touch/click, regardless
of camera yaw, elevation or zoom. The tactical overlay uses the inverse
projection through the same camera.

2D reference/debug mode remains available via the **2D** camera button.

## Controls

- Tap ROOK/MOSS, a soldier, or a numbered waypoint to select.
- Tap ground to add a movement destination. Drag nodes to edit.
- Selected node near a closed door: a contextual tray offers OPEN, KICK,
  HOLD and STACK without scrolling through unrelated tools.
- Select a movement node, tap STRAFE, then LOOK to set and hold an explicit
  world-space facing direction while that segment is traversed.
- Tap PIE ↶ / ↷, then tap **any valid pivot point** on the ground.
  The arc follows a circle of radius equal to the start-to-pivot distance;
  the soldier keeps their weapon toward the pivot. Select the pie node and
  drag the pivot marker to reposition or drag its end marker to set angle.
- BACK −1 removes one order; UNDO reverses editing gestures.
- ISO, TOP and 2D switch the view without resetting simulation or plans.
- ↶/↷ camera buttons rotate. Two-finger drag pans, twist rotates and
  pinch zooms. Desktop mouse wheel zooms. GO/PAUSE continues to work.

When a command is awaiting a map target, the floating toolbar is hidden so
it cannot intercept the user's target touch.

## Risk/acceptance tests

1. A waypoint beside a door presents the right contextual options even
   while the actual operator remains outside.
2. Contextual action chrome does not swallow target/map touches.
3. Free-pivot pies and custom sweeps never clip closed doors, walls or cover.
4. The authoritative player and AI decisions stay identical across ISO, TOP
   and 2D modes.
5. Unknown enemies remain hidden in every view, and remembered contacts
   stay approximate.
6. Camera rotation, selection, zoom and two-finger touch gestures do not
   accidentally add movement orders.
7. 3D soldiers and fixed geometry line up with the navigation collision
   rules and doors.
8. On a real portrait iPhone, both entering the station and editing
   multi-node plans remain easy and performance stays acceptable.

### Deliberate scope limit

This is a **first integrated implementation** across ten roadmap items.
The game still needs real player evaluation of movement feel, art quality,
occlusion behavior and enemy combat balance. We should not merge or claim
production polish based solely on tests and screenshots.
