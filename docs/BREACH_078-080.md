# Fieldwork Breach 078–080 — Tactical Presentation

This is a rendering and presentation pass on the existing fixed-step simulation.
The 2D plan remains the readability reference. No hit probabilities, damage
amounts, visibility rules, movement rules or AI decisions were changed.

## 078: clear 3D battlefield

- Removed the Three.js atmospheric distance fog that combined with fog of war
  to produce an opaque grey-green wash.
- Increased material separation between floors, walls, furniture and exterior.
- Added dark skirting at wall footings and completed the orthogonal floor grid
  (the original 3D grid contained only one axis of grout strips).
- Unknown cells use a strong dark, translucent overlay that leaves static
  architectural silhouettes legible (as they are in 2D). Dynamic enemy
  locations and door updates are visibility-gated separately, without the
  large opaque black ceiling seen in the first screenshot iteration.
  Explored cells receive a lighter dark tint; visible cells remain clear.
- Strengthened soldier contrast and helmet profile while keeping the chunky
  industrial miniature design.

## 079: actual combat event feedback

The simulation's ShotEvent now contains an ID, shooter ID, target ID,
before/after health and casualty result. Existing hits are generated by the
unchanged simulation; visuals never generate damage.

- A flash and rifle recoil now belong to the *actual shooter*, rather than
  any nearby same-team spark.
- 3D effects are pooled and keyed by the shot's ID. Repeated automatic fire
  reuses tracer and impact geometry and disposes short-lived effect materials.
- Actual hit results produce a flinch and a readable hit ring/label. A miss
  shows a dust-like impact at the resolved endpoint, never false target damage.
- On incapacitation the physical soldier turns toward a prone, grounded
  silhouette rather than shrinking to 33% height.
- Both 2D and 3D report HIT/DOWN consistently.
- Hidden shooters never leak their muzzle position or shot trajectory through
  fog; observable impacts can still be indicated.
- The independent /breach-visual-lab.html allows designers to preview
  synthetic MISS, HIT and DOWN presentation *without* starting a mission.
  Its events are clearly marked as dev examples and are not tactical shots.

## 080: doors and unified tactical affordances

- Doors now have stable 3D frames, visible thresholds, hinges, handles and
  moving leaves. Door geometry no longer vanishes when open.
- The 3D viewer retains the *last observed* door state when a door becomes
  unseen; opening elsewhere cannot reveal itself through the presentation.
- Closed/open state markers, tappable raised door cues and selected-door
  halos are visually distinct in ISO, overhead and 2D.
- Selection rings now project to actual ground coordinates, not arbitrary
  pixels beneath torso labels.
- Wounded, downed and suppressed cues use concise screen-space labels,
  remaining approximately the same size while zooming and orbiting.
- The introductory hint disappears after the player has begun the mission,
  reducing persistent text over the battlefield.

## Privacy of tactical information

The visual presentation is not authorized to reveal unseen combat events.
The new presentation helpers use the existing liveVisibility()/knownAt()
queries, and only actual observable shot endpoints or sources are shown.
Unseen dead hostile actors remain hidden until their location becomes visible.
