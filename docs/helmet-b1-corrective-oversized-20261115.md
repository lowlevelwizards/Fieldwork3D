# B1 — M1 bold silhouette corrective pass
## Observed mismatch
The previous 14-perimeter / eight-station M1 read as a small, detailed faceted dome while the approved ACH/MICH reads as larger, simpler slabs. The operator's head visually dominated the historical helmet. The rolled lip was too timid.
## Updated visual contract
- Eight perimeter vertices: four broad flat faces with four major connecting corners. No tertiary facets.
- Six contour stations: wide flat crown, one broad shoulder, long straight pot wall, broad raised lip and inward return.
- Full top height 8.98 model units; exterior bowl about 12.34 units wide before lip, with **13.76-unit lip width** and **14.24-unit maximum front-to-back lip projection**.
- Rolled edge thickness .33 units with projection >=.70 per side and >=.83 toward the front.
- Helmet remains simple, deliberately oversized and readable from standard tactical zoom; shell + fitted/loose fabric must share this silhouette.
- Field band, sparse coarse net, scrim and chinstrap follow new extents. Bare-shell and covered modes share the same contour logic.
- No retired model fallback, no new gameplay equipment/protection changes, no per-frame geometry.
## Geometry budget
Former shell: 14 roof triangles + 7×14×2 skirt triangles = **210 triangles**.
Corrected shell: 8 roof triangles + 5×8×2 skirt triangles = **88 triangles**, approx. **58% reduction**. Visual weight is concentrated in crown, long wall and rolled lip.
## Acceptance
1. Direct comparison with the user-supplied screenshots and approved ACH/MICH side + ¾.
2. Rim visually stronger at gameplay zoom, wall larger relative to operator head.
3. Covers do not conceal the rim or create a second top-tier cake.
4. Fixed tests pass and mobile Studio works; inspect screenshot artifacts before marking aesthetic acceptance.
