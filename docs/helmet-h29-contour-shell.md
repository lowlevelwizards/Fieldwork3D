# H29 — contour-driven ballistic shell

The H24–H28 ear mass system built several outward X-width rings, producing
long diagonal triangles and a visually attached ear box. H29 removes that
additional mass outright. The six existing front–temple–ear–rear–nape
hem stations remain the **only source of the cut silhouette**.

## Construction
- One unchanged chunky roof and upper crown chamfer.
- One planar side surface at the original crown's X half-width, continuing
  downward to 0.34 Fieldwork units above the existing lower edge.
- One short, fixed-height molded return from the planar side to each actual
  hem point, with less than 0.30 units of X movement for full/mid.
- Exactly the same front, ear, and nape Y/Z contour as H28; no edge spikes,
  lateral ear ridges, added attachment meshes, or per-frame updates.
- Full/mid coverage differs by the amount of downward shell extension;
  high-cut retains the clear negative-space arch and deep rear nape.
- Textile sides follow the exact same six hem points and 0.34-unit return.
  Fabric offset, PASGT atlas sampling, sewn-panel regions, and inward folded
  hem are unchanged.
- Rubber trim, chinstrap exits, four screws, band/rail/accessory placement
  remain driven by the established shared hem profile.
- Flat rectangular webbing and its four-point routing are unchanged.

## Acceptance
At normal mobile Studio angles and gameplay scale, the ear area should
look like the lower part of the same shell, not a block glued to its side.
No thin triangles spanning from ear panel to rear side. No clipping of
textile/trim, gaps, facial intersections, or new draw calls. All legacy
silhouette depth and cover/strap tests must remain valid after their
obsolete artificial-width assumptions are removed. Compare bare, covered,
front, side, rear, three-quarter and both realistic and fit angles.
